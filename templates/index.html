lonely 20 questions

you may be sad and have no friends but you can still play 20 questions.

powered by machine learning, yes, yet uses no LLMs & costs nearly nothing to run.

1 common english noun, 20 questions, ask yes or no questions

so how does this work?

this works by using the jev system one model from typesafe ai.

for every user 10 random nouns are picked from wonder words, jev decides whats the easiest to guess and uses that word.

every time you ask a question jev evaluates if question is yes or no, and if the question means you know what the word is.

we send those results back to the user, and that's the game.
